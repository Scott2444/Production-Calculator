# Calculation

This section explains how workflow rates are computed from project data and node configuration.

## What Gets Calculated

- Demand rates: ideal recipe rates required to meet target output rates
- Supply rates: actual recipe rates possible with your entered machine counts
- Product flows: inflow and outflow rates for product nodes
- Attribute totals: per-node demand and supply values for user-defined attributes

## Calculation Layers

The workflow calculation runs in ordered layers.

1. Pre-demand configuration: targets, preferred recipes, external product flags, and yield modifiers are collected.
2. Demand solve (LP): recipe rates are solved to satisfy target constraints and written as `calculatedTargetRate`.
3. Speed pass: solved recipe rates are converted into required machine counts as `calculatedMachineCount`.
4. Supply solve (LP): actual machine counts and node modifiers are used to solve achievable throughput as `calculatedActualRate`.
5. Attribute pass (frontend): node-level attribute values are calculated for demand and supply and then aggregated.

## Why Demand And Supply Are Separate

- Demand answers: "What should I build to hit my targets?"
- Supply answers: "Given what I already built, what can I actually produce?"

Keeping these separate lets you iterate safely on implementation without always reshaping the demand graph.
