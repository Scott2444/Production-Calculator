# Supply And Demand

Workflows track two distinct but linked calculations.

## Demand

Demand is target-driven:

- Uses project recipes and constraints
- Produces ideal rates and machine counts
- Determines the shape of your graph

## Supply

Supply is implementation-driven:

- Uses your entered machine counts and node settings
- Produces actual rates
- Represents your current build state

## Why The Distinction Matters

- Changing demand will morph the graph and potentially destructive of your current implementation
- Changing supply is a safe operation
