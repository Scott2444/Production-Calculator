# Product Nodes

Product nodes represent material flow state between process nodes.

## Typical Fields

- Product
- Calculated flow rate (inflow and outflow are equal)
- Actual inflow and outflow rates
- External provided

## Why Product Nodes Exist

- In most factory games, products for multiple sources are accumulated onto a single bus and the total throughput is desired
- It reduces the complexity of the workflow graphs. _Reduced edge count from NxM to N+M_

## Interpretation

- Less than ideal flow rate indicate bottlenecked supply
- Positive net flow rate indiciate accumulation of products, potentially leading to a backlog
